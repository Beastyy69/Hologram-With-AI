# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

Effective date: March 5, 2026

Our community is dedicated to providing a welcoming and respectful environment for contributors and maintainers. All participants are expected to show empathy, respect, and courtesy in all project spaces and interactions.

Standards
- Be considerate and respectful in communication and actions.
- Refrain from demeaning, discriminatory, or harassing behavior and language.
- Accept constructive feedback gracefully and offer it kindly.
- Follow repository contribution guidelines and respect maintainers' decisions.

Unacceptable Behavior
- Harassment, intimidation, or discrimination in any form.
- Offensive comments or images, including slurs and hateful language.
- Threats of violence or encouraging harmful activities.
- Repeatedly contacting someone who has asked to stop.

Reporting
If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, please report it by opening an issue addressed to the maintainers or by contacting the project owner(s) on GitHub. Include as much information as you can (screenshots, links, timestamps). Reports will be handled promptly and confidentially where possible.

Enforcement
Maintainers may take action for code of conduct violations, including warnings, temporary or permanent removal from the project, or notifying relevant parties. Decisions will be made by the maintainers based on the severity and context of the violation.

Scope
This code of conduct applies to project spaces (issues, pull requests, project boards) and public communication channels endorsed by the project.
